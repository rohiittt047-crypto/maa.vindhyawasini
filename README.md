## 2. Real E-Commerce & Booking Features

1. **Live Material Catalogue:**
   - Real material specifications with flexible bulk quantity units (CFT, Tractor Trolley, Truck Load, Kg, Tonne, 50kg Bags, 1000 Bricks).
   - **Dynamic Market Pricing (ताजा बाजार भाव):** In accordance with local construction supply standards where rates fluctuate daily, prices are handled on direct enquiry / call (`Rate on Enquiry / वर्तमान बाजार भाव पूछताछ पर`) rather than rigid hardcoded numbers.
   - Category filtering (All, Sand, Aggregates, Rod, Cement, Bricks, Hardware).
   - Real-time search and alphabetical sorting.

2. **Interactive Quantity Cart:**
   - Slide-over cart drawer with persistent `localStorage` synchronization.
   - Quantity selection by unit, minimum order quantity enforcement, and instant material tally.
   - Live badge counter in the navigation bar.

3. **Direct Checkout & Site Delivery Booking:**
   - Customer name, verified 10-digit phone number, delivery address & PIN code.
   - Delivery zone validation: Gangpur Siswan (Free local delivery), Siswan Block, Hasanpura, Raghunathpur, Siwan District.
   - Payment choices: Cash on Delivery / Shop Payment on verification, and UPI on Delivery.
   - Unique Order Reference generator (`MVT-2026-XXXX`).

4. **Order Tracking System:**
   - Real-time order lookup modal by Order ID or phone number.
   - Shows live order status and booked quantities.

5. **WhatsApp Order & Booking Bridge:**
   - Automatically generates a formatted material requirement breakdown sent directly to proprietor Ajit Kumar Singh at `+91 9304201774` to confirm current daily rates, loading, and vehicle dispatch.

6. **Bilingual Engine:**
   - Full English and Hindi support across catalog, cart, checkout, confirmation, and tracking.
